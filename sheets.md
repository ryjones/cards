# Sheets

The deck is four Letter-size sheets of nine cards each. Every sheet was
exported from the visual design tool as a standalone page (a `.dc.html`
artboard plus its runtime), and each export folder carries its own README
describing what is design and what is scaffolding.

Sheet 4 carries the title card, the full-alphabet reference card and the
"how to play" card alongside the last letters.

## Printing

The artboards are the design source. For printing, use the PDF export:
[Ukrainian Animal Alphabet Cards.pdf](Ukrainian%20Animal%20Alphabet%20Cards.pdf)
— print on card stock and cut along the dashed lines.
