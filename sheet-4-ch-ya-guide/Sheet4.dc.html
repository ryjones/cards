Як грати · How to play
1
Flash. Show the letter, say its sound, then flip to the animal. Repeat the word together.
2
Zoo line-up. Shuffle the deck and race to lay the animals out in alphabet order, using the reference card.
3
Guess the animal. Make the animal's noise or act it out; the other player finds the card and reads the word.
4
Sound hunt. Pick a card and find three more things in the room that start with that sound.
